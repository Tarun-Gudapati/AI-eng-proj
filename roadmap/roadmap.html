The rules

1. You type every line of practice code. AI explains, gives hints and reviews. Ask for the full answer ("show me") only after 10 minutes stuck, and then retype it yourself.
2. Say it out loud. After each topic, explain it in one minute without notes. If you can't, you haven't learned it yet.
3. Every Sunday, review the week. Tick it, and write three lines: what worked, what didn't, what's next.
4. Build small, then grow. Get one feature working end to end before adding the next.
